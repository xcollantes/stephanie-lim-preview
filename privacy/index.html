Privacy policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information I, Stephanie Lim, collect through this website and how I use it.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and the message you write.
  • Booking: if you book a call, the scheduling service collects the details you enter, under its own privacy policy.
  • Email: anything you send me directly.

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

How I use it

Only to reply to you, arrange sessions, and provide coaching. I never sell or rent your information, and I won’t add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Who else handles it

  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to my inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts my email.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and may process basic technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver it securely.

Coaching confidentiality

What you share in coaching is confidential. I follow the ICF Code of Ethics and won’t share anything about you, including with your employer, without your permission, unless the law requires it (for example, if someone is at risk of serious harm).

How long I keep it

I keep messages and coaching notes only as long as we’re working together and as required for legal or tax purposes, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information I hold about you at any time by emailing stephanielimcoaching@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
