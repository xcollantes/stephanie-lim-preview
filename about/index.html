About

Hi, I'm Stephanie

I’m Stephanie Lim, a life coach based in Seattle. I work with busy professionals, many of them in tech, who are brilliant at taking care of everything except themselves.

How I work

Coaching with me is warm and practical. We’ll get clear on what you want, notice the patterns getting in the way, and take small, real steps between sessions. You set the agenda; I bring the questions, the tools, and the accountability.

We can meet in person in Seattle, or by video or phone wherever you are.

Who I work with

  • Professionals feeling burned out or stretched thin
  • High achievers wrestling with self-doubt or imposter syndrome
  • Anyone at a crossroads: a new role, a move, a loss, or a “what now?”

Coaching isn’t therapy, and if I think a therapist would serve you better, I’ll tell you.

With gratitude, Stephanie
