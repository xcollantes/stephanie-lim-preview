Services

Life coaching services

Coaching for busy professionals who want to feel steadier, braver, and clearer about what's next. Meet in person in Seattle, or by video or phone from anywhere.

01

Burnout & balance

Get your energy back without blowing up your career.

When work takes everything, rest stops working and even the good parts of life start to feel heavy. Together we’ll find what’s really draining you and build a pace you can sustain.

What we work on

  • Spotting the early signs of burnout before they take over
  • Setting boundaries at work and at home, and keeping them
  • Letting go of the guilt that comes with rest
  • Building small daily habits that refill your energy

You’ll leave with a clearer sense of what matters, a calendar that reflects it, and the energy to enjoy your life again.

02

Confidence & self-trust

Quiet the inner critic and trust your own voice.

You’ve earned your seat at the table, yet a voice still says you don’t belong there. Coaching helps you understand that voice, loosen its grip, and act from confidence instead of fear.

What we work on

  • Imposter syndrome and perfectionism
  • Speaking up in meetings and asking for what you need
  • People-pleasing and the habit of over-explaining
  • Making decisions without endless second-guessing

You’ll leave trusting yourself more, saying what you mean, and showing up as the person you already are.

03

Life transitions

Find your footing and your next step when life changes.

A new role, a move, a breakup, a loss, or a quiet sense that it’s time for something different. Transitions are disorienting, even the ones you chose. Coaching gives you space to get clear and move forward with intention.

What we work on

  • Getting clear on what you want next, and why
  • Making big decisions with confidence
  • Navigating career changes, returns to work, and new chapters
  • Turning a vague “something needs to change” into a real plan

You’ll leave with clarity about your direction and a plan you actually believe in.

Frequently asked questions

How is life coaching different from therapy?

Therapy often focuses on healing the past and treating mental health conditions. Coaching looks forward: where you are now, what you want next, and the steps to get there. If something comes up that a therapist is better placed to help with, I'll tell you.

How do I know if coaching is right for me?

If you feel stuck, stretched thin, or ready for a change but unsure where to start, coaching can help. The free consultation is a no-pressure way to find out whether we're a good fit.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you want to work on. Lasting change usually takes a few months of regular sessions, and we'll decide together what makes sense for you.

What happens in a coaching session?

Each session starts with what matters most to you that day. We talk it through, get clear on what's really going on, and finish with one or two concrete steps to try before we meet again.

Do you offer online life coaching?

Yes. Alongside in-person sessions in Seattle, I coach by secure video call and by phone, so you can work with me from anywhere.

Can coaching help with burnout if I'm still working full-time?

Yes. Many people come to coaching while working full-time. Together we find what's draining you, set boundaries that stick, and rebuild your energy without having to quit your job.

Is coaching confidential?

Yes. What you share in our sessions stays between us, and that includes your employer. I follow the ICF Code of Ethics and never share details with anyone without your permission.
